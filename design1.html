How Home Swaps Work & Transparent Fees

Arranging a home swap used to be difficult, if not impossible. You not only had to find an available property in the area you wanted, but that property owner had to want to visit your area as well. Finding a needle in a haystack might be easier. But no more.

List Your Property

Contact Our Concierge

Our concierge staff's primary function is to help find you an exchange. They do a great job and best of all there is no additional charge for this coordinator service.

Finalize The Exchange

Once we find the property of your dreams, we put you in direct touch with the owner. Both of you agree on particulars, house guidelines, arrival dates, and key arrangements.
