Procedure of home interchange

Arranging a swap used to feel like
a needle in a haystack.

You not only had to find an available property in the area you wanted, but that owner had to want to visit your area at the exact same moment. But no more. Our dual Direct and Indirect models remove the friction entirely.

The exchange mechanisms

Two flexible ways
to travel.

Unlimited travel at affordable prices. Because every home is a second home, you are never locked into simultaneous arrival dates.

01 / DIRECT EXCHANGE ($250 FLAT)

Trade places.

You agree to a house swap with another member, and they have the choice of using your vacation house on the same dates or any other time convenient to you both. You pay only $250.00 for the entire vacation stay.

Keys and Arrival Logistics

Handoffs are simple: leave keys with your local property manager, in a secure lockbox with an access code, with a neighbor, or send an extra set via certified mail in advance.

Cleaning Protocols

Members agree on expectations together beforehand—including whether a local maid service fee is shared, how it's paid, and what to do with linens, sheets, and towels prior to departure.
