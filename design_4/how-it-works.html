How a swap works

Four steps from listing your home to picking up the keys.

List, search, message, stay

  1. Join

    List your vacation home with four or more photos.

  2. Search

    Type where you want to go and see matching homes.

  3. Message

    Write privately to members you like and agree on dates.

  4. Stay

    Pick up the keys and enjoy a real home.

Two ways to swap keys

Direct exchange

You stay in a member's home and they stay in yours. The dates don't have to match, so they can use your place whenever suits you both.

$250 for your whole vacation

Indirect exchange

You stay in a member's home even if they never use yours. They earn an Exchange Credit to put toward their own next swap.

$500 a week, $72 a night for longer stays

Questions

Is home swapping safe?

You message members privately and agree on every detail before any exchange is confirmed.

When do I pay?

Only when you arrange an exchange.

Do the dates have to match?

No. Direct exchange dates do not need to be simultaneous.

What if the other member does not want my home?

Choose an indirect exchange. They earn an Exchange Credit instead.
